2. Privacy Policy (UK GDPR & Data Protection)

Last Updated: November 2026

2.1 Data We Collect

  • Studio Account Data: When you register on BloxCloud, we collect your developer email address, chosen username, studio name, and a salted cryptographic hash (`scrypt`) of your password.
  • Game Server Telemetry: Connected Roblox game servers transmit non-personal operational metrics including server `JobId`, physics FPS, memory usage, script error stack traces, and numerical Roblox `UserId` / display username values solely for matchmaking queues and in-game trade locks.
  • Billing Data: All payment card and PayPal details are processed directly by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy. BloxCloud never sees or stores your credit card number.

2.2 How We Use & Protect Data

Your data is used strictly to provide, secure, and isolate your studio's cloud infrastructure. Each studio account is logically partitioned so other studios cannot view or modify your servers, flags, matchmaking queues, or marketplace listings.

2.3 Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), you have the right to access, export, or request complete erasure of your studio account and associated data at any time by contacting us via our official X account (@Bloxcloud1999).

3. Billing, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Merchant of Record: Lemon Squeezy

3.1 Subscriptions & Taxes

Paid plans (Studio Pro £24/mo, Scale Studio £79/mo, and Top Games Enterprise £199/mo) are billed monthly in advance through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as our Merchant of Record and handles global sales tax, UK VAT, and EU VAT compliance automatically.

3.2 Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your Lemon Squeezy billing receipt link or the BloxCloud Plans & Billing panel. When you cancel, your studio retains full access to your paid tier features until the end of your current paid billing period, after which your account automatically transitions to the Free tier without data loss.

3.3 Refunds

Because we offer a permanent Free Tier so studios can test every module before upgrading, monthly subscription charges are generally non-refundable once the billing cycle has begun. However, if you experience a verified platform outage or accidental duplicate charge within 14 days of payment, contact us via @Bloxcloud1999 and we will issue a full refund through Lemon Squeezy.
